01 · Introduction

Privacy comes first

At FINVERO, your financial privacy is fundamental.

FINVERO is a personal finance management application designed to help users organize and understand personal finances, including income, expenses, savings, budgets, goals, lending, debt, recurring payments, subscriptions, accounts, transfers and financial reports.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FINVERO may collect, how it is used, where it is stored, which service providers may process it, how it is protected and how users can request deletion.

04 · My Vault

Highly sensitive personal information

My Vault is designed as a local-only feature. Vault information is intended to remain on your device and is separate from normal FINVERO cloud financial data.

Vault information may include

  • Bank account details
  • Mobile financial account information
  • Payment card information
  • Identity and document information
  • Document images
  • Card number
  • Expiry date
  • CVV
  • Card PIN
  • MFS PIN
  • Vault security-question answers
Local-only design: Vault information is not intended to be uploaded to Firebase, stored in Firestore or Firebase Storage, synchronized to the FINVERO cloud, included in normal financial exports, sent to advertising services, sent to AI services or sent to third-party OCR services.

Vault Security

My Vault is designed to use encrypted local storage and Android security mechanisms, including Android Keystore-backed protection where supported by the implementation.

Biometric or device authentication may be required before sensitive Vault information can be revealed.

Vault Data Loss

Because Vault data is local-only, it may be lost if the application is uninstalled, device data is erased, the device is factory-reset, the device is lost or damaged, or encrypted local data becomes inaccessible.

05 · Firebase & Google Services

Services used to operate FINVERO

Firebase Services

Depending on enabled functionality, FINVERO may use:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring
  • Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Cloud Functions

Firebase Authentication

FINVERO may use Email/Password authentication and Google Sign-In. Authentication providers may process information required to authenticate and maintain your account.

Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise

FINVERO may use Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise or Firebase authentication protection mechanisms to help detect automated or abusive activity during applicable login, registration and password-reset operations.

reCAPTCHA-related processing may involve technical information needed for security and abuse prevention.

Push Notifications

FINVERO may use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver recurring-payment reminders, subscription reminders, financial alerts, account-related notifications and important application notifications.

FINVERO does not intentionally include sensitive Vault secrets such as full card numbers, CVVs, PINs or passwords in push notifications.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may be used to understand application usage and improve usability, stability, performance and product development.

FINVERO is designed not to intentionally send full card numbers, CVVs, PINs, passwords or Vault secrets as analytics event parameters.

Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics may process technical information such as device information, operating system information, application version, crash information, stack traces and technical diagnostics.

Firebase Performance Monitoring

Performance Monitoring may process technical information related to application startup, screen loading, network performance, requests and application/device performance.

06 · Advertising

Google AdMob

FINVERO may display limited advertising using Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob.

Google advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, application information, advertising interactions and other technical information according to their applicable policies and settings.

Financial privacy: FINVERO does not intentionally provide advertisers with bank account numbers stored in My Vault, card numbers, CVVs, PINs, passwords, private Vault documents or detailed personal financial records.

FINVERO does not intentionally use full card numbers, CVVs, PINs, passwords, Vault secrets or private document contents for personalized advertising.

Third-Party Providers

FINVERO may rely on third-party technology providers for authentication, cloud synchronization, notifications, analytics, crash diagnostics, performance monitoring, abuse prevention and advertising.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

07 · Security

Protecting your information

FINVERO uses security measures appropriate to its application architecture.

  • HTTPS/TLS for network communication
  • Firebase authentication controls
  • User-specific database access controls
  • Firestore security rules
  • Android application security mechanisms
  • Encrypted local storage for My Vault
  • Android Keystore-backed protection where supported
  • Biometric/device authentication for sensitive Vault operations
  • Server-side validation where applicable
  • Access restrictions for user-owned financial records
No system is perfectly secure. No internet-connected, electronic or local storage system can guarantee absolute security.
08 · Retention & Deletion

How long information is kept

Data Retention

FINVERO retains account and financial information for as long as necessary to provide the requested service, maintain the account, meet legitimate operational requirements or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Some technical information, crash records or analytics information may be retained by third-party providers according to their respective retention policies.

Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their FINVERO account and associated cloud data through the deletion pathway provided by FINVERO.

Depending on the implementation, deletion may cover applicable account information and cloud-stored financial records, including transactions, income, expenses, salary, savings, goals, lending, debt, recurring payments, subscriptions, accounts/wallets and transfers associated with the account.

My Vault is separate. Because My Vault is designed as local-only storage, cloud account deletion does not automatically erase local Vault information from your Android device.

External Account/Data Deletion Request

If you cannot access the application, you can submit an account/data deletion request by contacting:

Request FINVERO account/data deletion by email

Please provide enough information to identify the relevant account without sending unnecessary passwords, PINs, CVVs or other sensitive information.

Certain information may be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, regulatory compliance or legitimate technical operations.
