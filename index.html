Data Privacy & Transparency

FINVERO Privacy Architecture

At FINVERO, your financial privacy is fundamental. This policy transparently explains what data is collected, how it is stored and processed, what services are integrated, and the controls you have over your personal information.

Version: 1.0.0 Effective: September 6, 2026 Updated: September 6, 2026
Zero third-party advertising trackers or data brokers.
01 · Information

Information We Collect

FINVERO collects only the information necessary to provide personal finance tracking, budget calculations, financial organization, and everyday money-management features.

Account Information

When you create and use an account, FINVERO may process account-related information such as:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Authentication-related identifiers

Authentication is securely managed through Firebase Authentication.

Financial Data & Transactions

FINVERO processes financial information that you voluntarily enter into the application, which may include:

  • Income records
  • Monthly salary configurations
  • Expense records
  • Transaction categories
  • Payment methods
  • Transaction dates and times
  • Transaction descriptions or notes
  • Savings information
  • Savings goals
  • Debt information
  • Lending information
  • Budget information
  • Recurring payment schedules

Payment method selections may include options such as Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Bank, or Cards where supported by the application.

App Preferences & Local Cache

FINVERO may store certain application preferences and locally cached information on your device to provide a responsive and reliable experience.

  • Selected currency
  • Budget warning preferences
  • Reminder preferences
  • Notification-related preferences
  • Offline/pending synchronization data
  • Other application settings
02 · Usage

How We Use Your Information

Information processed by FINVERO is used primarily to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the application's financial-management functionality.

Financial Calculations

Financial records may be used to calculate wallet balances, income and expense summaries, cash flow, category spending breakdowns, savings progress, budgets, lending balances, debt balances, and related financial summaries.

Safeguards & Alerts

FINVERO may use your financial records and preferences to provide budget warnings, balance-related safeguards, recurring payment reminders, and other application notifications.

Access Security

FINVERO uses authenticated access controls and database security mechanisms designed to ensure that your financial documents are accessible only to the authorized account.

Service Reliability

Technical and diagnostic information may be processed to identify crashes, performance problems, synchronization failures, and other technical issues that affect the reliability of FINVERO.

03 · Integrations

Third-Party Services & Integrations

FINVERO utilizes industry-standard cloud infrastructure and supporting services to provide authentication, database, synchronization, notifications, analytics, diagnostics, and application performance capabilities where enabled.

Google Firebase — Authentication & Cloud Firestore

Firebase Authentication manages user authentication and account sessions. Cloud Firestore provides cloud database storage and synchronization for application data. FINVERO's Firestore security architecture is designed to isolate user records according to authenticated ownership.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Where enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to deliver application notifications and relevant reminders to supported devices.

Firebase Analytics

Where enabled, Firebase Analytics may be used to understand general application usage and improve product reliability and usability. FINVERO does not intentionally send unnecessary sensitive financial transaction details as analytics events.

Firebase Crashlytics

Where enabled, Crashlytics may process crash and diagnostic information to help identify and resolve application failures.

Firebase Performance Monitoring

Where enabled, Performance Monitoring may collect technical performance information to help identify slow or unreliable application experiences.

What We Do NOT Use: FINVERO contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no cross-site advertising trackers, and no sale or leasing of financial data to data brokers, lenders, or marketing partners.

04 · Security

Data Storage, Security & Isolation

Per-User Data Isolation

FINVERO is designed around authenticated user ownership. Financial records such as transactions, salary settings, savings, debts, budgets, lending records, and recurring payments are associated with the appropriate authenticated user account.

Firestore Security Rules are designed to prevent unauthorized users from reading, modifying, deleting, or overwriting another user's documents.

Encryption in Transit & at Rest

FINVERO communicates with supported cloud services through encrypted network connections using HTTPS/TLS.

Data stored through Google Cloud/Firebase infrastructure is protected using Google's standard security and encryption mechanisms for data at rest.

Offline Data

Where offline functionality is supported, FINVERO may temporarily retain locally cached or pending synchronization information on the device so that supported actions can continue without an active internet connection.

Security Realism

FINVERO applies reasonable security practices involving authentication, authorization, database rules, encrypted communications, and supported cloud security controls. However, no electronic transmission, application, device, or cloud storage method can be guaranteed to be 100% impenetrable.

Users are responsible for maintaining secure login credentials and protecting access to their devices and accounts.

05 · Data Control

Data Portability, Retention & Deletion

FINVERO is designed to give users meaningful control over the financial information they enter into the application.

Data Export

Where the export feature is available, users can export supported transaction information to CSV format for their own records and personal use.

Clear Financial Data

Where supported by the application, users can clear or reset their financial records without necessarily removing their authentication account.

Data Retention

Financial and account-related information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested services, maintain application functionality, support synchronization, protect the Service, prevent abuse, resolve technical or operational issues, and satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Data Deletion Requests

FINVERO does not currently provide an in-app permanent account-deletion mechanism. If you have a question about your personal information or wish to request assistance regarding applicable data deletion rights, please contact KX Technologies using the official support email provided below.

06 · Children

Children's Privacy

FINVERO is designed as a general personal finance management application.

We do not knowingly design FINVERO to solicit personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child or minor has provided personal information in circumstances where such collection is not permitted by applicable law, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

07 · Updates

Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in FINVERO's features, technology, data practices, security architecture, or applicable regulatory requirements.

When this policy is updated, the Last Updated date will be revised accordingly. Significant changes may also be communicated within the application where appropriate.

Continued use of FINVERO after an updated policy becomes effective indicates acknowledgment of the updated policy, subject to applicable law.

Questions & Inquiries

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your data rights, data handling, or data deletion requests, you can contact the FINVERO team directly.
